\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}
Large language model interfaces increasingly expose controls that appear legible to end users. This paper asks whether a readable cognitive axis is also a reliable behavioral control primitive.

\paragraph{Motivation and scope.}
Mishra et al.'s non-surjectivity result motivates an internal prompt/latent gap \cite{mishra2026nonsurjective}. We treat that result as theory background, not as evidence that prompting is behaviorally impossible.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
The contribution list must remain tied to \texttt{docs/paper/claim-map.yaml}.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{C1, exploratory measurement:} a representational-facade measurement on Qwen2.5-7B where readable prompts reach only a fraction of the extracted pole for 3/4 axes; Llama replication remains pending.
  \item \textbf{C2, headline result:} under a frozen adjudicator, naive CAA/ITI steering fails to beat the bounded best-prompt ceiling in all tested method--model cells and harms uncertainty calibration in all four cells.
  \item \textbf{Methodology asset:} a reusable frozen prompt-vs-latent behavioral adjudication protocol.
  \item \textbf{C3, design implication only:} cognitive consoles should instrument limits and trust-calibration risks rather than promise latent control. No empirical user-study credit is claimed on this route.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Design Implications}
\label{sec:design-implications}
C3 is non-empirical on the pure-model route. The design implication is to treat the console as a limits and trust-calibration instrument, not as evidence that users can control latent states.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
C1 is exploratory and single-model pending Llama replication. C2 is scoped to bounded prompt effort and naive/off-the-shelf CAA/ITI. The result is not an impossibility theorem. The calibration-harm mechanism is open, and the human-study fork remains owner-gated.

\section{Future Work}
\label{sec:future-work}
Future work includes the frozen latent-recovery arm for PSR-style DEV-optimized steering, Llama C1 replication, a possible owner-approved human study, and a new preregistered mechanism package for calibration harm.
