# Provisional Novelty-Deflation Defense

## Direct Neighbors Already Cover

- AxBench: method-level comparison between prompting and representation-level interventions.
- Basu et al.: representation-to-actionability gap, including a prompt-engineering arm.
- Bo et al.: latent-steering interfaces, an unscaffolded prompt baseline, and exploratory user evidence.
- Golden Gate Claude: public visibility for internal-feature amplification.

All neighbor descriptions remain provisional until citation metadata, full text, and adjacent-claim support are verified.

## Components That Are Not Novel Alone

- DEV/TEST separation;
- comparator declaration and matched items;
- preregistration and frozen thresholds;
- bootstrap uncertainty and multiplicity correction;
- effect-size margins;
- coherence/safety checks;
- distinguishing absence of evidence from equivalence;
- evidence scoping by model, method, and task;
- warning against extrapolating from interpretability to control.

The paper cannot claim novelty for placing five familiar fields on a card.

## Remaining Candidate Contribution

A per-affordance **evidentiary entitlement rule** that binds permission to expose a latent control to:

1. a declared bounded behavioral comparator;
2. a prespecified outcome and superiority margin;
3. a coherence condition;
4. explicit computational statuses and blocking reasons; and
5. a non-transferable evidence scope.

The five-field contract carries the computational result into structured evidence and then a record-specific interface action. It does not add a formal state taxonomy or displace the comparator-bound eligibility criterion as the conceptual center.

## Kill Conditions

The novelty defense fails if:

- prior work already formalizes the same affordance-level eligibility gate;
- applying the rule does not change a concrete interface decision beyond an ordinary evaluation report;
- the pending prompt+steer study makes the substitution-based worked application irrelevant to active controls.

Comparator fairness is not established, and human evaluation of the interface mapping remains absent. These boundaries constrain the claim to a study-specific bounded comparator and a proposed computational eligibility procedure without assay or HCI-effectiveness validation.

## Source-Level Update — 2026-08-11

- **AxBench verified:** direct prompting/finetuning/representation-method benchmark; no interface affordance qualification rule found in official PDF search.
- **Basu v1 verified:** 400 vignettes, four mechanistic arms, safety-focused prompt condition, and parser sensitivity shift 0.451→0.729; no comparator-bound interface eligibility artifact found.
- **Bo v3 verified:** three interfaces, PROMPT baseline, n=14 user study, and a computational prompt-on-top-of-steering experiment. This makes composition a required construct check, not a novel contribution.
- **Karny full text verified:** *Neural Transparency* (IUI 2026, DOI 10.1145/3742413.3789120) already provides a passive mechanistic-interpretability interface and an N=80 between-subjects user study. The visualization increased endpoint trust and perceived helpfulness but did not measurably change design behaviors/outcomes or behavioral-prediction confidence. Active steering interfaces are future work. The remaining novelty candidate is therefore not “first interpretability interface” or “first human evidence,” but an affordance-level rule for when evidence licenses an **active control**, with an explicit behavioral comparator and scoped state.

### Closest-Neighbour Consequence for the Planned Human Study

The pending study should not merely ask whether a neural-evidence visualization is liked or increases trust; Karny already supplies that comparison and shows subjective/behavioral divergence. To contribute new evidence, the study must test the distinctive record-to-action claim—for example, whether comparator-bound qualification changes control-granting decisions, whether people distinguish a blocking reason from a pass, or whether they understand why a read-only diagnostic remains a candidate while an active control is withheld. Exact questions and outcomes remain pending in `HUMAN_STUDY_PENDING.md`.

The provisional novelty is now narrower: a comparator-bound, evidence-reason-aware **eligibility record for a specific active-control affordance**, instantiated by a preregistered executable procedure and worked application. Full-text neighbor review supports the narrow distinction; absent human evidence means the paper must present the mapping as a proposed method, not as a demonstrated improvement to interface decisions.
