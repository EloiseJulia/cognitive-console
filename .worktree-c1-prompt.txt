5. PROMPT shift: for each HELD-OUT POS readable prompt, prompt_shift = project_scalar(act, û) − neutral_proj. strongest_prompt_shift = max over held-out POS (the best a readable prompt does). Also report the mean.
6. facade_ratio = strongest_prompt_shift / ||v||  (signed). Random-direction NULL: reuse the metrics null baseline to test whether strongest_prompt_shift clears the p95 of projections onto random unit directions.
